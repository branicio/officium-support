Privacy Policy

Effective July 21, 2026

Officium collects no data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account system, and no developer-operated network service. All liturgical content, calendar data and read-aloud audio are bundled with the app and work offline.

Purchases

Subscription purchase and restore actions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's privacy policy. Support, legal and review links may open Apple services or your browser; Officium does not receive data from those visits.
