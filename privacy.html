Privacy Policy

Effective August 12, 2026 · supersedes the version of July 21, 2026

Officium collects no data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account system, and no developer-operated network service. All liturgical content, calendar data and read-aloud audio are bundled with the app and work offline. This policy sets out, in detail, what that means.

What Officium collects

Nothing. Officium holds no name, no email address, no account, no advertising identifier and no device fingerprint. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and operates no server of ours. We cannot see who you are, what you pray, how often you open the app — or whether you have opened it at all.

What stays on your device

The app keeps a small set of preferences in its own private storage on your device so that it works the way you left it:

  • your language (English, Portuguese or Spanish) and psalm translation choice;
  • reading preferences — text size, Latin/vernacular arrangement, appearance;
  • reading positions and playback speed, so an hour reopens where you left it;
  • whether the welcome tour and reading tips have been shown.

These preferences never leave your device — there is nowhere for them to go. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases and subscriptions

The Officium Complete subscription is offered, charged and renewed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). Your payment details, Apple Account information and billing history are handled entirely by Apple and are never shared with us. The app verifies its entitlement on the device itself. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Links that leave the app

A few actions open something outside the app: this support site, this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, and the option to rate Officium on the App Store. Those pages open in your browser or in the App Store under their own terms; Officium receives no data from those visits. This site itself is a set of static pages served by GitHub Pages, with no analytics of ours.

Third-party services

None, beyond Apple's own platform services described above. Officium embeds no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no cloud services. The prayer texts, the 2026–2035 calendar and the audio recordings ship inside the app bundle.

Your prayer is private

What you read, pray and listen to in Officium stays on your device. We keep no record of the hours you open, the psalms you linger on, or the devotions you keep — by design, no such record exists anywhere.

Children's privacy

Officium collects no personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, there is nothing a parent or guardian could ask us to review or delete.

Your data rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data a developer holds about you. Officium holds none, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase: deleting the app from your device removes everything it ever stored. If you have any question about this, write to us — we answer personally.

Changes to this policy

If Officium ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated first and the effective date above revised. Since the app has no way to contact you, this page is the single place to check.
