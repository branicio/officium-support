Privacy Policy

Effective July 21, 2026

Officium collects no data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account system, and no developer-operated network service. All liturgical content, calendar data and read-aloud audio are bundled with the app and work offline.

Purchases

Subscription purchase and restore actions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's privacy policy. Some links in the app — the support page, this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, and the option to rate Officium — open in your browser or in the App Store; Officium does not receive any data from those visits.
