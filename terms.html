2. The App

Officium presents the traditional Roman Breviary — the 1962 typical edition, under the rubrics of 1960 — with the correct office resolved for every day from 2026 through 2035, together with the Little Office of the Blessed Virgin Mary. Texts appear in Latin side by side with English, Portuguese or Spanish, and the Little Office can be read aloud. Everything works offline. The App is licensed to you, not sold.

3. A devotional aid, not an official edition

Officium is prepared with great care: its texts come from established public-domain editions and the daily structure is verified against the Divinum Officium project. Clergy and religious bound to the Office should satisfy their obligation from their proper books and ordo; laypeople may pray from Officium with confidence, and we welcome corrections at the address below.
