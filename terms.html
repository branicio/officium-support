Subscription

“Officium Complete” is an auto-renewable subscription ($2.99/month or $9.99/year in the U.S. storefront) that unlocks the seven subscription hours of the Roman office — Matins, Lauds, Prime, Terce, Sext, None and Vespers — and the complete Little Office of the Blessed Virgin Mary (all eight of its hours). A two-week free introductory trial is available to eligible new subscribers; Apple determines eligibility. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when an eligible trial ends, or at confirmation of purchase if no trial applies. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Roman-office Compline remains free without a subscription.

Content

Liturgical texts are public-domain editions; structural data derives from the Divinum Officium Project (MIT License). Read-aloud audio of the Little Office is generated in-house from those public-domain texts. Sources are listed inside the app under Settings → Content Sources. The texts and audio are provided for prayer and study without warranty of fitness for liturgical obligation.
