# Officium — Support

Support site for **Officium: 1962 Roman Breviary**, an iOS app for praying the
traditional Roman Breviary (1962 typical edition) — Latin and English, side by
side, correct for every day and fully offline.

## Links

- **Support website & FAQ:** https://branicio.github.io/officium-support/
- **Privacy Policy:** https://branicio.github.io/officium-support/privacy.html
- **Terms of Use:** https://branicio.github.io/officium-support/terms.html

Every page is trilingual — English, Brazilian Portuguese and Latin-American
Spanish — switched by the EN / PT / ES tabs in the hero. A `#en` / `#pt` / `#es`
URL fragment deep-links straight into a language (it also wins over the
remembered choice); these are the URLs the app uses:

- **Privacy Policy (PT):** https://branicio.github.io/officium-support/privacy.html#pt
- **Privacy Policy (ES):** https://branicio.github.io/officium-support/privacy.html#es
- **Terms of Use (PT):** https://branicio.github.io/officium-support/terms.html#pt
- **Terms of Use (ES):** https://branicio.github.io/officium-support/terms.html#es
