Frequently asked

  • Which rubrics does Officium follow? The 1960 rubrics (1962 typical edition), the office prayed alongside the 1962 Missale Romanum. Structure is verified against the Divinum Officium project.
  • Is Compline really free? Yes — free forever, no account needed.
  • What does the subscription unlock? The other seven hours — Matins, Lauds, Prime, Terce, Sext, None and Vespers — every day from 2026 through 2035, fully offline.
  • Modern or traditional English? Both. Choose in Settings → Reading → Psalm translation: modern English (default) or the traditional Douay-Rheims.
  • How do I cancel? Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Your access continues to the end of the paid period.
