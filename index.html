Officium

The Traditional Divine Office

Officium is the traditional Roman Breviary — the 1962 typical edition, under the rubrics of 1960 — with the correct office resolved for every day, Latin and English side by side, and everything working offline. Alongside it prays the Little Office of the Blessed Virgin Mary, the layperson's office of Our Lady, which you can also hear read aloud.

What's inside

✠All eight hours

Matins, Lauds, Prime, Terce, Sext, None, Vespers and Compline — the complete daily cursus of the 1962 Roman office, for every day from 2026 through 2035.

✠The Little Office of the B.V.M.

The complete Officium Parvum in its own tab — the traditional lay office of Our Lady, with its seasonal variants for the whole year.

✠Listen & follow along

Every hour of the Little Office can be read aloud, fully offline, with verse-by-verse highlighting that follows the voice, adjustable speed, and lock-screen controls.

✠Latin & English

The Clementine psalter side by side with English — or either alone. Choose modern (Catholic Public Domain Version) or traditional (Douay-Rheims).

✠A traditional ordo

The calendar shows each day's celebration, class and liturgical colour, with home-screen and lock-screen widgets for today's feast.

✠Offline & private

No account, no ads, no tracking, no developer server. All prayer texts, audio and the calendar are bundled with the app. Compline is free forever.

Frequently asked
Which rubrics does Officium follow?
The 1960 rubrics (1962 typical edition) — the office prayed alongside the 1962 Missale Romanum. The daily structure is verified against the Divinum Officium project.
Is Compline really free?
Yes — the Roman-office Compline is free forever, with no account needed.
What does the subscription unlock?
The other seven hours of the Roman office — Matins, Lauds, Prime, Terce, Sext, None and Vespers — and the complete Little Office of the Blessed Virgin Mary, every day from 2026 through 2035, fully offline.
Can I listen to the Little Office?
Yes. Every hour can be read aloud, fully offline, with verse-by-verse highlighting and adjustable playback speed.
Modern or traditional English?
Both. Choose in Settings → Reading → Psalm translation: modern English (default) or the traditional Douay-Rheims.
Does Officium need internet access?
No. Prayer texts, audio and the 2026–2035 calendar are bundled and work without a developer server. Purchasing or restoring a subscription uses Apple's StoreKit service; support, legal and review links open Apple services or your browser.
How do I cancel?
Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Your access continues to the end of the paid period.
